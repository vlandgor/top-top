How to play

L R
One button per boot. Pressing a boot swings it one tile around the other, which stays put
↻ ↺
The left boot turns clockwise, the right one counterclockwise. Alternate them to walk
▣
The dashed tiles show where each button sends its boot. If the edge is in the way, the boot stays put and both boots turn around
■
Red is deadly. Sentries step on the beat; big sweeps glow first and only kill when solid
Left side or A / ← presses the left boot. Right side or D / → the right one. R restarts, Esc pauses.
