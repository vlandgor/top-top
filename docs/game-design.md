# Top Top: game design (v0.1)

*Working title in early notes: Lockstep / Heel to Heel.*

A minimalist mobile puzzle-arcade game. You don't steer a character: you steer a pair of
boots that are always neighbours, side by side or diagonal, never further apart.

## Movement

Based on Chick Mates (`applyJump` in chick-mates-sandbox):

- **Two buttons, one per boot.** Pressing a boot swings it one place (45°) around the other
  boot, which stays put as the anchor.
- **Fixed directions.** The left boot always turns clockwise, the right one always
  counterclockwise. Alternating presses walks the pair; pressing one boot repeatedly turns
  the pair in place.
- **At the edge:** if the next place is off the board, the boot steps **back** one place along
  its circle instead. Nothing flips; the step back makes room for the other boot to swing into
  its old spot. (On any board of at least 2×2, one step back always finds a tile.)
- **Facing:** each boot always faces along its own path: the next tile ahead, even when that
  is off the board. A step back is a step backwards, never a turn around, so the boots never
  swap sides. Where a press sends the boot is outlined with the button's letter (L / R), or
  `L↩` / `R↩`, fainter, when it is a step back.

## Time

The game is deterministic, in **ticks** of 125 ms. Hazards step once per **beat** (4 ticks by
default, 120 bpm; per level). The player can press at most once per tick; a press during a
used tick is buffered to the next one. (Chick Mates is turn-based; Top Top keeps its own beat
clock so hazards move in real time.) The clock starts when the level loads, like
Geometry Dash; dying restarts the level in about 0.4 s (or at once on the next tap).

`src/game/rules.ts` holds these rules as pure functions. The game and the solver both run
them, so whatever the solver proves, a player can do.

## Hazards

- **Sentries:** glowing tiles that walk a fixed path, one tile per beat, looping or
  ping-ponging. The tile they step to next is outlined, brighter as the step comes closer.
- **Sweeps** (grid-wide events): a front crossing the board lane by lane: down, up, left,
  right, diagonal ("waterfall"), anti-diagonal, or the whole board at once ("pulse"). Each tile
  glows for `warn` beats, then is lethal for `lethal` beats (default 1). Sweeps can repeat,
  and leave **gaps** (safe columns, rows or diagonal channels) that can change every pass.

## Modes

| Mode | Accent | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Destination | teal | Get either boot onto the goal. |
| Collect All | coral | Pick up every light. |
| Sequence | purple | Pick up the numbered lights in order. Touching the wrong one restarts. |
| Survival | red | Stay alive until the clock runs out. |
| Waves *(in progress)* | blue | A repeating sweep leaves lights behind it; the next pass washes away what's left. Collect enough of them. |

## Levels

20 levels per mode, 100 in all, generated by `tools/gen-levels.ts` on a smooth difficulty
curve:

- **Destination, Collect All, Sequence:** levels 1–3 have no hazards (walking, the edge turn);
  sentries arrive at level 4 (slow beat, one at a time), more sentries and loops follow, and
  sweeps arrive around level 14–15 on their own before being mixed in. Boards grow from 6×4
  to 11×7; Collect and Sequence go from 2 to 6 lights.
- **Survival:** sentries crossing where you stand (1–4), whole-board pulses with wide gaps
  (5–8), pulses with a sentry (9–12), waterfalls (13–16), then everything mixed. The clock
  goes from 14 to 40 beats.
- **Waves:** slow waves with wide gaps first; the gap narrows, waves come faster and closer
  together, and a sentry joins from level 15. Lights needed: 3 to 7.

Fairness rules for every level: nothing reaches a standing player for 3 s (2 s after level
10), and hazards always block the easy route. Every level ships with a proof that it can be
beaten at a human pace. The first level of each mode, and the first level with each new
hazard, shows a tip.

The first level of each mode is open; clearing one opens the next (in the dev build too). The game is landscape only (either way up), with a boot button on each side of
the board.

## Co-op rooms

Inspired by the Grid rooms at Activate: 2–4 players on one floor, playing short challenges
together. Solo stays exactly as it is; rooms are a separate menu entry (**Co-op**) with their
own levels (`src/levels/rooms.ts`).

- **Every player steers their own pair** with the solo movement and buttons. Pairs never
  block each other; they just overlap. Each pair has its own colour (you are always teal).
- **One team:** the team shares a pool of **lives**: a room's `lives` (5 by default) are for
  two players and grow in proportion (8 for three, 10 for four). A pair that dies loses one,
  sits out 1 s, then comes back at its start once nothing will hit it there for 1.5 s. The
  run is lost when the lives run out (or, in Collect, the **clock**: `limit`, in beats).
- **Flow:** lobby → 3-2-1 countdown → play → results (team time, lives left, each player's
  lights and falls). Pausing counts back in from 3. The team's best time is kept per room and
  player count.
- **Room levels** list one start per player (`starts: ["ax,ay bx,by", …]`) instead of A / B on
  the map, plus `lives`, `limit` and `more` (extra lights for a 3rd and a 4th player). There is
  no proof: the tests prove a room by having bot teams clear it at every player count and
  skill. Regular and Pro teams must win every run; a Rookie team at least half.
- **The lobby** has a room per mode, picked with tabs in the mode's colour.

| Room | Mode | Co-op rule |
|---|---|---|
| *Light Hunt* | Collect All | Lights are shared: any player's pickup counts. 13 lights (16 for three players, 19 for four), 2 sentries, 60 s. |
| *Hot Floor* | Survival | Stay alive together for 44 s: the room is cleared with any lives left. Pulses whose safe columns swap sides, two sentries, and from halfway a wave down the board. |

Still to come: Sequence (one shared order, a wrong light costs a life instead of restarting),
Destination (every pair has to reach the goal; a pair that does is home and stops), Waves (a
shared count).

### Bots

Until there is online play, bots fill the other seats, so the whole flow can be built and
tested solo. In the lobby each of the three extra slots is Open or a bot: Rookie, Regular or
Pro (easy / normal / hard in the code).

A bot steers its pair through the same rules a person does, one decision per tick. It looks
5 s ahead with a breadth-first search over its own pair against the real hazard schedule,
heads for the soonest light that another bot hasn't claimed and that a teammate isn't clearly
closer to, and rethinks when that light is gone. With nothing to collect (Survival) it takes
the safe route with the fewest presses, so it holds still when it can; when no route is safe,
the one that lasts longest. Skill is three knobs: press pace (5 / 4 / 3 ticks: 625 / 500 /
375 ms), reaction time before acting on a new goal, and how often a press goes to the wrong
boot (3% / 2% / 0%), which can cost the team a life like a person's slip. Everything random
is seeded, so a match replays the same way.

### Toward online play

The rules (`src/game/room.ts`) don't know who presses: the local player, bots and later
remote players all go through `pressPair()`, at most once per tick per pair. The game is
deterministic, so online play can be lockstep: send presses tagged with their tick, not
positions. The lobby's open slots are where friends will join.

## Still open

- Obstacles from Chick Mates (trees, boxes, gates) are not in yet; the board edge is the only
  thing that blocks.

- Waves: tuning, more levels, whether it needs a time limit.
- Achievements screen.
- Co-op: rules for the other modes (shared sequence, team survival, everyone to the goal),
  more rooms, lights and lives scaled by player count, versus rules, then online play.
- In-app level editor with sharing. The level format is plain data and the solver can
  validate player-made levels before they're shared.
- Practice mode or checkpoints; how progression and saves should work.
- Whether swings should snap to the beat for a stronger rhythm feel.
